# ELECTRIC VEHICLE PERFORMANCE AND INSIGHTS
Electric Vehicle Performance and Insights is a data analytics project developed using Tableau to explore and analyze the electric vehicle market. It transforms a large dataset into meaningful visualizations and interactive dashboards to understand EV sales, manufacturers, market regions, vehicle categories, battery types, and performance indicators.
# Project Overview
The electric vehicle industry is growing rapidly, making it important to understand market trends, sales performance, and customer preferences through data analysis.
This project uses the Electric Vehicle Market Classification Dataset to analyze approximately 200,000 records containing information about electric vehicle sales, manufacturers, model years, battery technologies, pricing, driving range, efficiency, and customer ratings.
Tableau Desktop was used to connect the dataset, create visualizations, develop calculated fields, and build an interactive dashboard. A Tableau Story was also created to present the analysis in a structured and meaningful sequence.
The final outcome is an interactive dashboard that provides a clear overview of electric vehicle market performance and key insights.

# Project Overview
The main objectives of this project are:
1. To analyze electric vehicle sales across different manufacturers.
2. To compare EV sales performance across various market regions.
3. To understand the distribution of electric vehicles by category.
4. To identify sales trends across different model years.
5. To analyze the contribution of different battery types to EV sales.
6. To evaluate vehicle performance using indicators such as price, driving range, efficiency, and customer ratings.
7. To create interactive visualizations that make complex data easier to understand.
8. To develop a centralized dashboard for exploring important EV market insights.
9. To use calculated fields and KPIs to generate meaningful analytical information.

 # Dataset Overview
 The project uses the Electric Vehicle Market Classification Dataset, containing approximately 200,000 records and 37 fields.
The dataset provides information about electric vehicle manufacturers, sales, categories, battery technologies, pricing, vehicle specifications, and customer ratings.
It was imported into Tableau Desktop through a CSV connection and used as the primary data source for creating worksheets, KPIs, and the interactive dashboard.
## Key Dataset fields
Vehicle Id -Unique identifier for a vehicle record     
Manufacturer- Name of the EV manufacturer     
Market Region -Geographical market or region      
Category -Classification of the electric vehicle      
Model Year -Manufacturing or model year     
Annual Sales Units- Number of vehicles sold annually     
Battery Type -Type of battery technology used     
Price USD -Vehicle price in US dollars      
Range Km -Estimated driving range in kilometres      
Efficiency Wh Per Km - Energy consumption per kilometre        
Customer Rating -Customer rating of the vehicle         
Acceleration 0 100 Sec -Time taken to accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h
# Interactive Dashboard
An interactive dashboard titled EV Market Performance Dashboard was developed using Tableau Desktop.
The dashboard combines multiple visualizations and key performance indicators into a single interface, allowing users to explore different aspects of the electric vehicle market.
## A. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Four KPI views were created to provide a quick summary of the dataset:
Total EV Sales: Displays the total annual sales units.
Average EV Price: Shows the average vehicle price in USD.
Average Driving Range: Represents the average driving range in kilometres.
Average Customer Rating: Displays the average customer rating.
## B. Data Visualizations
Six worksheets were developed to analyze different aspects of the EV market:
Worksheet Chart Type Purpose    
EV Sales by Manufacturer- Horizontal Bar Chart- Compares sales across manufacturers   
EV Sales by Region -Treemap -Shows the distribution of sales across regions    
EV Sales by Category -Horizontal Bar Chart- Compares sales across EV categories      
EV Sales by Model Year -Line Chart -Identifies year-wise sales trends    
EV Sales by Battery Type- Packed Bubble Chart- Visualizes sales by battery technology      
EV Performance Analysis -Vertical Bar Chart- Supports comparison of EV performance indicators
## C. Calculated fields 
Three calculated fields were created to enhance the analysis:
1. Sales Contribution %
Calculates the percentage contribution of a dimension's sales to the overall sales total, depending on the table calculation settings.
2. Average Sales per Vehicle
Calculates the ratio of total annual sales units to the distinct vehicle IDs.
3. EV Customer Rating Status
Classifies customer ratings into three categories:
High Rated
Moderately Rated
Low Rated
These calculated fields help provide additional analytical information beyond the original dataset fields.
## D. Interactive Filters
Two interactive filters were added to the dashboard:
Market Region: Allows users to view data for selected geographical regions.
Model Year: Allows users to explore sales and performance data for specific model years.
These filters make the dashboard dynamic and allow users to customize the analysis according to their requirements.
## E. Dashboard Layout and Design
The dashboard was arranged in a structured layout with four KPI views at the top and six visualizations organized below them.
A consistent title and layout were used to make the dashboard professional, readable, and easy to navigate.
## F. Tableau Story
A Tableau Story containing seven story points was created to present the project findings step by step:      
Manufacturer Sales Analysis      
Regional EV Sales Analysis     
Category-wise EV Sales Analysis      
Year-wise EV Sales Trend       
Battery Type Sales Analysis       
EV Performance Comparison      
Final EV Market Dashboard       
The Story provides a guided presentation of the analysis, beginning with individual market comparisons and ending with the complete dashboard.
